## Service Model

DAZ Sentinel is a specialist service line of **DAZ Consulting DS**, combining **industrial cybersecurity**, **PMC advisory**, **project controls**, **planning**, **forensic analysis**, **claims support**, and **risk management** for critical infrastructure, energy, petrochemical, industrial and EPC environments.

We support organisations across the full asset and project lifecycle — from **concept, FEED and EPC delivery** to **commissioning, handover, operations, dispute resolution and recovery programmes**.

Engagements are structured to produce outcomes that remain defensible over time: **scope, evidence, decisions, accountability, controls and follow-through**.

---

## PMC Advisory & Project Governance

### Purpose

Large industrial projects require more than technical execution. They require governance, accountability, decision discipline, interface control, reporting transparency and an operating model that can withstand delivery pressure.

DAZ Consulting DS supports Owners, investors, contractors and leadership teams in establishing or improving the governance model for complex EPC and industrial projects.

### Where it fits

- **Concept / FEED / Basic Design**: project strategy, execution model, decision gates, interface risks
- **EPC / Construction**: contractor coordination, progress governance, reporting discipline, issue escalation
- **Commissioning / Handover**: readiness control, documentation maturity, punch-list governance, operational transition
- **Recovery / Dispute Phase**: delay analysis, evidence reconstruction, claims strategy and project recovery planning

### What you receive

- PMC governance model aligned with the project lifecycle
- project execution control framework
- Owner / EPC / Contractor / Vendor responsibility matrix
- decision-making and escalation structure
- interface control and reporting model
- project board and steering committee reporting structure
- recovery roadmap for delayed or distressed projects

## Project Controls & Planning

### Purpose

Schedule, cost, scope, progress and risk must be managed as one integrated control system. Without reliable project controls, management decisions are based on incomplete, inconsistent or misleading data.

This service establishes, audits or improves the project controls environment so that project status, forecasts and risks are measurable, traceable and defensible.

### What you receive

- baseline schedule review and planning maturity assessment
- Primavera / MS Project schedule logic review
- critical path and near-critical path analysis
- progress measurement system assessment
- S-curve and physical progress control model
- earned value and cost-schedule integration recommendations
- reporting structure for project leadership and stakeholders
- early warning indicators for delay, cost overrun and execution risk

## Forensic Schedule & Cost Analysis

### Purpose

When a project is delayed, disrupted or commercially exposed, management needs more than standard reporting. It needs forensic reconstruction of what happened, when it happened, who caused it and what consequences followed.

This service provides evidence-based analysis for internal decision-making, negotiations, claims, expert opinions, mediation, arbitration or litigation preparation.

### What you receive

- forensic schedule analysis
- delay event reconstruction
- critical path impact assessment
- disruption and productivity loss analysis
- cost impact mapping
- causation analysis between events, delays and financial consequences
- evidence register and document chronology
- defensible analytical narrative for management, lawyers or external experts

## Engagement Format

- **Discovery** — scope, constraints, contract structure, project status, evidence sources and decision requirements
- **Assessment** — engineering, governance, schedule, cost, claim, risk or security assessment depending on the engagement type
- **Verification** — reality check against documents, configurations, schedules, correspondence, progress records, site conditions and operational behaviour
- **Analysis** — evidence-based findings, causation logic, risk impact, exposure mapping and management implications
- **Embedding** — governance, controls, reporting structures, claim-support logic or handover-resistant operating practices
- **Executive Output** — clear management-level conclusions, decision points, recommended actions and defensible documentation
