Our work connects **project delivery**, **project governance**, **project controls**, **planning and scheduling**, **claims and forensic analysis**, **industrial cybersecurity**, **OT / ICS governance**, **DCS / SCADA systems**, **PLC engineering**, **industrial networks**, **virtualisation**, **backup**, **maintenance support** and **secure operational infrastructure**.

## Forensic Schedule, Cost & Claims Support

### Purpose

Claims require more than legal argument. They require a coherent evidentiary record, reliable chronology, clear causation logic, technical substantiation, contractual alignment and defensible quantification of time and cost consequences.

In delayed and disputed EPC projects, the effectiveness of a claims strategy depends on the ability of the engaged team to operate with discipline, flexibility, persistence and high technical competence over an extended period of pressure. The process is vulnerable to internal weaknesses such as fragmented documentation, inconsistent communication, weak coordination, delayed decisions and loss of evidence, as well as external pressure from contractors, vendors, counterparties, advisers and other third parties.

This service supports management in maintaining control over the forensic and claims process: validating assumptions, structuring evidence, protecting causation logic, aligning technical and contractual arguments, and converting project disruption into a defensible, executable and commercially coherent analytical position.

### What you receive

- claim submission validation against contractual procedures, professional guidelines and evidentiary requirements
- preliminary assessment of entitlement, causation, impact mechanism and evidentiary sufficiency
- verification of claim completeness: notices, event description, chronology, affected obligations, supporting records and requested relief
- forensic schedule analysis based on validated assumptions and project records
- delay event reconstruction and critical path impact assessment
- disruption and productivity loss analysis
- cost impact mapping and quantum support structure
- event chronology and evidence mapping
- evidence register, document chronology and issue-based claim file structure
- technical claim narrative preparation
- analysis of contractor or client claims
- support for negotiation, mediation, arbitration or litigation preparation
- defensible analytical narrative for executive decision-making, legal advisers or external experts
