Firm profile

DAZ Consulting DS operates in capital-intensive industrial environments where schedule integrity, cost performance, contractual discipline, operational readiness and secure technical infrastructure are critical to project and asset performance.

The practice combines project governance, project controls, planning and scheduling, forensic schedule and cost analysis, claims support, risk management, industrial automation engineering support and OT / ICS cybersecurity advisory. The objective is to support decisions, controls and technical actions that remain defensible under real project, plant and operational conditions.
